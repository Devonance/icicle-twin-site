Credits and licences

Icicle Twin (https://devonance.github.io/icicle-twin-site/) is built on other people's work: Microchip's published design files for the Icicle Kit, open-source software, and a few freely licensed assets. This page lists every third-party library, asset, data source, quoted excerpt and tool, with its licence and where it comes from. The live site shows this same file as its credits page (credits.html, linked from the About card), with the full licence texts of the code bundled into its JavaScript.

Everything not listed here (the app's code, the 3-D models built by our own Blender scripts, the simulator, the cards, lessons and glossary) is Icicle Twin's own, under the licences below.

Icicle Twin's own licences

Icicle Twin is open source, © 2026 Kevin Horton. Microchip's Icicle Kit design files (the board data)

The board in the twin is Microchip's own design: every outline point, footprint, pad, copper track, via, net, reference designator and BOM row comes from the design files Microchip publishes for the Icicle Kit (PCB 20-0532 Rev 1.0). They are © Microchip Technology Inc. The raw files are kept in the private repository and never published; the site ships data derived from them (data/board/ and the tables in data/), credited to Microchip (Kevin's decision, docs/QUESTIONS_FOR_KEVIN.md, answer 2). Icicle Twin is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microchip.

Parts checked against datasheets and Kevin's photos

Every package model is built by our own script (tools/blender/packages/) from the maker's datasheet dimensions and the board's own footprints, then checked against photos of Kevin's own kit (docs/DIMENSIONS.md, docs/QA_PHOTO_ROUND2.md). Each written part card names and links the datasheets its facts come from (Microchip, Würth Elektronik, Texas Instruments, Micron, E-Switch, onsemi, Coilcraft and the other makers in the BOM); facts only, no text reproduced. Kevin's photos are reference material and are not published.

3-D assets: searched first, none used

Kevin's order for 3-D assets is BlenderKit (CC0 or Royalty Free only), then Poly Haven or ambientCG (CC0), then our own Blender script. BlenderKit, Poly Haven and ambientCG were searched for every part family, the peripherals and the finale's monitor (results in tools/blender/**/research/, docs/ASSETS.md); no model matched a real part, so none is used. Meshy was looked at for form only and no Meshy model is shipped. The one stock asset is the Poly Haven HDRI above (assets/packages/CREDITS.md).
